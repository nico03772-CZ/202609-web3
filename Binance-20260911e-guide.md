## What Is Grid Trading?

Grid Trading is an automated strategy that places multiple buy and sell orders at equal intervals across a set price range. When price drops to a lower grid line, the system buys; when it rises to an upper line, the system sells. You profit from repeated swings inside the range without needing to predict the trend. ## Grid Trading Risks and Notes

- **Grid is not universal**: It works best in sideways markets; if price breaks out of the range in one direction, you may take losses or hold only one side.
- **Only use money you can afford to lose**: Fund the grid with idle capital, never borrowed funds.
- **Set a sensible range**: Too narrow gets swept and stuck; too wide yields few fills.
- **Beware scams**: Anyone asking you to move coins to a private wallet or promising guaranteed gains is a scammer.
