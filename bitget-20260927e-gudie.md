## 1. First, Understand How Bitget's Fees Work

Before cutting fees, know how they're charged. Bitget's rates are shaped by three variables:

1. **Order role: maker or taker**
   - **Taker**: market orders or actively filling orders that "take" liquidity from the book — higher rate.
   - **Maker**: resting limit orders that wait to be filled, adding liquidity — lower rate.
   - Pick the wrong order type on the same trade and the fee gap is substantial.

2. **VIP tier**
   Hitting trading volume or asset thresholds upgrades your VIP level, lowering rates step by step. Less relevant for small accounts short-term, but worth tracking for large, active users.

3. **Product line**
   Spot, futures, and copy trading each have their own structures; futures add funding rates (periodic settlements between longs and shorts) — not technically a fee, but it hits your holding cost all the same.
