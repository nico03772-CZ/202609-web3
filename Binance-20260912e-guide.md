## What Is Binance Wealth Management

Binance wealth management (Binance Earn) is the platform's idle-asset appreciation service. In short, you put crypto you aren't actively trading into platform products and earn returns under set terms, rather than leaving funds idle in your spot wallet.

Wealth management is different from contract trading: contracts profit from price movement, while wealth management leans toward locked savings or staking yields — with different risk profiles and logic.

## Common Binance Wealth Management Products

Typical products in the Binance wealth management section include:

- **Flexible savings**: deposit and withdraw anytime, interest accrues daily — good for short-term idle funds.
- **Fixed / locked savings**: assets locked for a set term, usually with a higher APY than flexible, redeemable at maturity.
- **Staking**: stake tokens to participate in network validation or liquidity and earn on-chain or platform rewards.
- **Liquidity mining / structured products**: such as dual investment, with more complex payoff structures suited to experienced users.
