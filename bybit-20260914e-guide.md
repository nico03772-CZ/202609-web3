## What Is a Deposit

A deposit moves crypto from an external wallet or another exchange into your Bybit account. Only after a successful deposit can you trade spot, contracts, and other products.

The key point: a deposit is one-way — once sent and confirmed on-chain, a mistake is almost impossible to reverse. So extra care is needed on your first deposit.

## What First-Time Depositors Should Watch

### 1. Pick the right coin and chain (network)
Bybit supports many coins, and a single coin often has multiple chains (e.g., USDT can arrive via ERC20, TRC20, BEP20…). The chain you choose must match the sender. Sending on the wrong chain can mean permanent loss.

### 2. Copy the correct deposit address
Each coin has its own deposit address on Bybit. Copy the address from Bybit's "Deposit" page and paste it into the sender. Don't type it manually to avoid character errors.

### 3. Check the Memo / Tag (if required)
Some coins (like XRP, XLM, EOS) require an extra Memo or Tag on deposit. Missing it may prevent the deposit from being credited. Always confirm whether Bybit asks for this field.

### 4. Start with a small test amount
For your first deposit, send a small amount to confirm the process, then deposit larger sums afterward. This catches mistakes at the lowest possible cost.

### 5. Wait for on-chain confirmations
Deposits aren't instant — they need a number of blockchain confirmations. Timing depends on network congestion and the coin. Don't panic if it doesn't show immediately.

### 6. Mind minimums and network fees
Some coins have a minimum deposit; below it, the transaction may not be credited. Also, the network fee (gas) is paid by the sender, not collected by Bybit.
