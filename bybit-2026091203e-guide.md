## What Is a Bybit TradFi Spread Contract

A Bybit TradFi spread contract (often called a CFD — Contract for Difference) is a derivative contract that tracks the price of traditional finance assets. You don't need to actually own Tesla shares, gold, or a dollar index; with this contract, you profit from or absorb losses based on the underlying asset's price movement.

In short: TradFi spread contracts bring traditional-market moves — US stocks, indices, commodities, forex — into a crypto trading platform, settled in USDT. This lets crypto users participate in traditional finance price action without opening a traditional brokerage account.

## What Underlyings Can You Trade

Common Bybit TradFi underlyings include:

- **US stocks**: popular tickers like Tesla, Apple, Nvidia (tracked via contract, not share ownership).
- **Indices**: major indices such as Nasdaq and S&P 500.
- **Commodities**: gold, silver, crude oil, and more.
- **Forex**: price moves of major currency pairs.

These contracts typically trade on USDT margin, support a degree of leverage, and run on a 24/5 schedule (Monday to Friday, nearly around the clock) that mirrors traditional market hours.
