## What is OKX spot margin?
OKX spot margin lets you borrow assets from the platform's margin pool to trade on the spot market with leverage, with available multiples varying by pair (up to around 10x). Unlike futures, spot margin still fills on the real spot market and you hold the underlying asset, but you can:
- **Long**: borrow USDT to buy coins, then sell and repay after a price rise.
- **Short**: borrow coins to sell for USDT, then buy back and return after a drop.
Borrowed funds accrue hourly interest, and you must maintain a sufficient margin ratio or face liquidation.
