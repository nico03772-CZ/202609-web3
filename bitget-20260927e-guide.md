## 6. Common Beginner Mistakes

1. **Screenshotting the seed phrase**: one phone infection wipes everything — the most common self-custody accident.
2. **Sharing the seed with "support"**&#8203;: official support never asks for it; every request is a scam.
3. **Cross-network transfers**: withdrawing on the wrong chain sends funds to a network the wallet can't show — recovery is very hard.
4. **Signing blind approvals**: connecting to unknown DApps and confirming can grant permission to move your assets.
5. **Forgetting which chain you're on**: multi-chain labels differ; not checking the network is like mailing a package to the wrong floor.
