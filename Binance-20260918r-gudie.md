Withdrawal sends assets to an external wallet address (self-custody wallets like Trust Wallet, MetaMask). It carries a **network fee** charged by the blockchain.

1. Go to **Wallet → Withdraw → On-Chain**.
2. Choose the asset (e.g., USDT) and the network (ERC20, BEP20, TRC20, etc.).
3. Paste the recipient wallet address; make sure the network matches the destination wallet.
4. Enter the amount, review the displayed withdrawal fee, and confirm with 2FA.
5. After on-chain confirmation, assets reach the destination wallet; timing depends on network congestion.
