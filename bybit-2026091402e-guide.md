## What Is the Bybit Unified Trading Account

The Unified Trading Account (UTA) is an account mode on Bybit that lets you trade multiple products — spot, contracts, and options — within a single account, instead of opening separate sub-accounts for each market.

Its biggest difference from the classic mode: previously each market had its own margin wallet, whereas UTA pools assets into one "unified margin pool" shared by all products.

## What Is Shared Margin

Shared margin means your asset balance acts as collateral for all your positions in spot, contracts, and options at the same time. Specifically:

- **Spot, contracts, and options share one margin pool**: You don't need to move coins into separate wallets per market.
- **Capital efficiency**: Spot profits can offset contract losses (and vice versa), improving overall fund utilization.
- **Centralized risk management**: All positions are shown under one unified margin ratio, giving you a clear account-wide view.

## Benefits of the Unified Trading Account

1. **Higher capital efficiency**: No more fragmentation across wallets, reducing idle funds stuck in one market that can't cover another.
2. **Flexible position switching**: Diversifying between spot, contracts, and options is smoother, with no manual transfers.
3. **Unified risk management**: Monitor your entire account risk from a single screen.
